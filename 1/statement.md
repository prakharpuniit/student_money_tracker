# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often receive money from parents, scholarships, part-time work, or other sources and spend it on food, travel, college requirements, shopping, and entertainment. Without a simple tracking system, it can be difficult to know where the money is going.

The Student Money Tracker provides a simple Python-based solution for recording income and expenses, checking balance, managing a budget, and tracking savings.

## Scope
The project focuses on basic personal finance management for students. It records financial transactions and provides simple reports and budget information.

## Target Users
- College students
- School students
- Students receiving monthly allowances

## High-Level Features
1. Income management
2. Expense management
3. Transaction history
4. Budget management
5. Savings goals
6. Financial reports
7. Data storage
8. Input validation
9. Testing
